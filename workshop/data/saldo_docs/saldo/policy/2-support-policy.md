## 1. How to reach support

| Channel | Availability |
| --- | --- |
| In-product help | Always |
| Email — support@saldo.example | Always; answered within support hours |
| Telephone | Professional and Enterprise plans, within support hours |
| Named contact | Enterprise plans only |

Every contact creates a case. Cases carry an identifier in the form `CAS-NNNNN`.

## 2. Severity

Severity is set by Saldo on the facts of the case, not by the customer's plan. Customers may
challenge a severity; disputes go to the duty manager.

| Severity | Definition | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S1 — Critical | A service is unavailable, or a payroll cannot be completed for any customer | Web application down; disbursement halted |
| S2 — High | Significant impairment with no workaround, affecting a payroll deadline | A customer cannot approve a run before cut-off |
| S3 — Medium | Impairment with a workaround, or a subset of records affected | Some rows rejected; a report fails to render |
| S4 — Low | Question, cosmetic issue, or change request | How-to questions; documentation gaps |

Note that **a payroll run completing with rejected rows is normally S3**, even when the customer
is under time pressure. It becomes S2 if the rejections prevent the customer from meeting a
statutory deadline and no correction run is possible in time.

## 3. Response and resolution targets

Time to first meaningful response, within support hours for the plan:

| Severity | Standard | Professional | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | 4 hours | 1 hour | 30 minutes |
| S2 | 8 hours | 4 hours | 2 hours |
| S3 | 2 business days | 1 business day | 4 hours |
| S4 | 5 business days | 3 business days | 2 business days |

Resolution targets are not contractual. Saldo commits to continuous effort on S1 and S2 until a
workaround or fix is in place.

## 4. Escalation

1. **Within the case** — ask the assigned engineer to escalate. This raises visibility, not
   severity.
2. **Duty manager** — for disputed severity or a missed response target.
3. **Head of Customer Operations** — for repeated failures or a relationship at risk.

Enterprise customers may escalate directly to their named contact at any point.

An escalation is recorded on the case. It does not by itself change the severity, the target,
or any entitlement under the *Service Level Agreement* (SALDO-DOC-0040).

## 5. What support will and will not do

Support **will**: investigate, explain what happened, identify the cause, propose a fix, and
prepare correction runs on request.

Support **will not**: change employee data on the customer's behalf without written
instruction, approve a payroll run, or confirm a service credit entitlement. Credit claims are
assessed under SALDO-DOC-0040 section 5 by Commercial, not by support.
