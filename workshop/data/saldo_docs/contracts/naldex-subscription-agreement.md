## 1. Services

Saldo provides Naldex with access to Saldo on the **Standard** plan, for use in Naldex's own
business.

Saldo is cloud accounting software. Naldex's finance team uses it to keep its books, run payroll,
and reconcile against its bank accounts. Saldo provides the system and applies the statutory
rules; the work is done by Naldex.

The subscribed modules are listed in Schedule A. The subscription covers the web application and
the SFTP channel, for entities and employees in the Czech Republic and Slovakia. The API is not
included on the Standard plan.

The scope of the service, and what falls outside it, is set out in the Service Description
(SALDO-DOC-0001), which forms part of this agreement. Commercial details are set out in
Schedule A.

## 2. Term and Renewal

This agreement runs for **12 months** from the date it comes into force.

It then renews automatically for successive 12-month periods, unless either party gives three
months' written notice before the end of the current period.

## 3. Fees and Payment

Naldex pays a monthly subscription based on the number of active employees in the account, at the
rate set out in Schedule A. Saldo invoices monthly in arrears, with payment due within 30 days.

Additional services — off-cycle payroll runs, expedited processing, custom reports — are charged
separately at the rates in Schedule A.

Correction runs are not charged. Where a payroll run leaves employees unpaid and those employees
need to be run again within the same pay period, Saldo makes no charge, regardless of how many
correction runs are required.

Saldo may revise its prices once in any 12-month period, giving three months' notice. If Naldex
does not accept the revised prices, it may terminate this agreement with effect from the date they
would take effect.

## 4. Service Levels

Saldo commits to the availability targets set out in its Service Level Agreement. For the Standard
plan this is **99.5% per calendar month**.

**The Standard plan does not include service credits.** Where Saldo fails to meet the target,
Naldex's remedy is the right to terminate under clause 10, not a credit against its invoice.
Service credits are available on the Professional and Enterprise plans.

Payments rejected because the submitted data did not meet Saldo's published file specification are
the service operating as designed. Such rejections do not count towards unavailability.

## 5. Support

Naldex receives support at the Standard level: business hours, 09:00–17:00 CET on working days,
**by email**. Telephone support is available on the Professional and Enterprise plans and is not
included here.

Support outside those hours is charged at the rate in Schedule A.

Response targets by severity are set out in Saldo's Support Policy. Saldo assigns severity based on
the facts of each case. Where Naldex disagrees with an assigned severity, it may refer the matter
to the duty manager.

## 6. Responsibilities

Saldo is responsible for operating the service, maintaining its availability, processing submitted
data in accordance with the published file specification, and transmitting valid payment
instructions to its banking partner within the agreed timescales.

Naldex is responsible for the accuracy of the data it submits, including employee bank account
details, and for submitting it in time.

Saldo pays what Naldex instructs it to pay. Saldo validates that submitted data is well formed —
that an account number is a valid IBAN, that an amount is a positive figure — and rejects anything
that is not. Saldo cannot verify that a correctly formatted account belongs to the intended
recipient. Only Naldex is in a position to know that.

Where an employee is paid late because of an error in their record, correcting that record is
Naldex's responsibility. Saldo will assist: support can prepare corrected files for Naldex to
review, and correction runs are not charged.

**Submission deadlines.** Payroll submitted after the cut-off published in the Payroll Run Guide
(SALDO-DOC-0008) is processed for the next available value date. Saldo makes no commitment about
the payment date of a late submission, and a payment that is late only because the run was
submitted late is not a failure of the service. Where Naldex needs a late submission paid on the
original date, expedited processing is available at the rate in Schedule A.

## 7. Changes to the Service

Saldo may change the service during the term, provided it does not reduce the functionality Naldex
subscribes to without agreement.

Where a change requires Naldex to take action — updating data, altering an integration, or changing
the format of a submitted file — Saldo gives at least **60 days' notice**. Notice is given by email
to Naldex's nominated technical contact.

## 8. Data Protection

Naldex's data remains its own. Saldo processes it solely to provide the service, and for no other
purpose. Saldo does not sell it and does not use it to train machine learning models.

Saldo acts as processor and Naldex as controller. The data processing agreement is attached as
Schedule B.

Payroll records are retained for seven years as required by Czech law. All other data is deleted
within 90 days of this agreement ending. Naldex may export its data at any time, including after
termination.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Neither party limits its liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or
for any other liability that cannot be limited by law.

Subject to that, each party's total liability in any 12-month period is limited to the fees paid
under this agreement during that period.

Neither party is liable for loss of profit, loss of business, or reputational harm.

## 10. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement immediately if the other:

- commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of written notice, or
- becomes insolvent.

Naldex may also terminate if Saldo fails to meet the availability target in three consecutive
calendar months.

On termination, Saldo refunds any subscription paid in respect of a period after the termination
date.

## 11. General

This agreement is governed by Czech law, and the courts of the Czech Republic have exclusive
jurisdiction.

It constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes any prior representations or
arrangements.

No variation is effective unless made in writing and signed by both parties.
