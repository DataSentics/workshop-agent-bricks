## 1. Services

Saldo provides Bartoň a partneři with access to Saldo on the **Professional** plan, for use in
Bartoň a partneři's own business.

Saldo is cloud accounting software. Bartoň a partneři's staff use it to keep its books, issue and
chase invoices, file its returns, and reconcile against its bank accounts. Saldo provides the
system and applies the statutory rules; the work is done by Bartoň a partneři.

The subscribed modules are listed in Schedule A. The subscription covers the web application, the
SFTP channel and the API, for entities and employees in the Czech Republic and Slovakia.

**Use for third parties.** This agreement licenses Saldo for Bartoň a partneři's own accounts. It
does not license the provision of bookkeeping, payroll or filing services to third parties using
Saldo — bureau use — and it does not license the keeping of a third party's books in Bartoň a
partneři's own tenant.

Bartoň a partneři is a tax advisory and bookkeeping practice, and Saldo understands that it acts
for client companies. Where Bartoň a partneři wishes to use Saldo for that work, the arrangement is
one of the following:

- each client company holds its own Saldo subscription, and grants Bartoň a partneři access to it
  as its adviser, at no additional charge to Bartoň a partneři; or
- Bartoň a partneři enters into Saldo's separate **Bureau Agreement**, which provides for multiple
  client entities under one account, per-entity pricing, and the controller and processor
  relationships that arrangement requires.

Neither is provided for by this agreement. This clause is to be reviewed at each renewal.

The scope of the service, and what falls outside it, is set out in the Service Description
(SALDO-DOC-0001), which forms part of this agreement. Commercial details are set out in
Schedule A.

## 2. Term and Renewal

This agreement runs for **24 months** from the date it comes into force.

It then renews automatically for successive 12-month periods, unless either party gives three
months' written notice before the end of the current period.

## 3. Fees and Payment

Bartoň a partneři pays the monthly subscription set out in Schedule A. Saldo invoices monthly in
arrears, with payment due within 30 days.

Payroll is not subscribed, so no per-employee charge arises. Should Bartoň a partneři subscribe to
Payroll during the term, it is charged per active employee at the list rate for the Professional
plan then in force.

Additional services — custom reports and the other items in the Fee Schedule — are charged
separately at the rates in Schedule A.

Saldo may revise its prices once in any 12-month period, giving three months' notice. If Bartoň a
partneři does not accept the revised prices, it may terminate this agreement with effect from the
date they would take effect.

## 4. Service Levels

Saldo commits to the availability targets set out in its Service Level Agreement. For the
Professional plan this is **99.9% per calendar month**.

Where Saldo fails to meet that target, Bartoň a partneři may claim a service credit against its
next invoice. Credit amounts and the claim process are set out in the Service Level Agreement.

Service credits compensate for the service being unavailable. They do not compensate for a missed
filing deadline, whether Bartoň a partneři's own or a client's.

## 5. Support

Bartoň a partneři receives support at the Professional level: extended hours, 07:00–19:00 CET on
working days, by email and telephone.

Response targets by severity are set out in Saldo's Support Policy. Saldo assigns severity based on
the facts of each case. Where Bartoň a partneři disagrees with an assigned severity, it may refer
the matter to the duty manager.

Saldo's support covers the operation of the service. It does not extend to advising on the
accounting or tax treatment of a transaction, whether Bartoň a partneři's own or a client's.

## 6. Responsibilities

Saldo is responsible for operating the service, maintaining its availability, processing submitted
data in accordance with the published file specification, and transmitting valid payment
instructions to its banking partner within the agreed timescales.

Bartoň a partneři is responsible for the accuracy of the data it submits and for the correctness of
anything it files from the service.

Saldo applies the statutory rules in force as it has implemented them. Whether a return is correct,
complete and filed on time is Bartoň a partneři's responsibility, as is the professional advice it
gives its own clients. Saldo is not Bartoň a partneři's adviser and is not its clients' adviser.

## 7. Changes to the Service

Saldo may change the service during the term, provided it does not reduce the functionality Bartoň
a partneři subscribes to without agreement.

Where a change requires Bartoň a partneři to take action — updating data, altering an integration,
or changing the format of a submitted file — Saldo gives at least **60 days' notice**. Notice is
given by email to Bartoň a partneři's nominated technical contact.

Changes required by amendments to Czech tax law are released as soon as they are ready and are not
subject to the notice period, since the statutory date is not Saldo's to move.

## 8. Data Protection

Bartoň a partneři's data remains its own. Saldo processes it solely to provide the service, and for
no other purpose. Saldo does not sell it and does not use it to train machine learning models.

Saldo acts as processor and Bartoň a partneři as controller in respect of Bartoň a partneři's own
data.

Where personal data relating to a third party's employees or customers is present in the account,
the controller is that third party and not Bartoň a partneři. This agreement does not establish the
chain of processing that such an arrangement requires. See clause 1.

Records are retained for seven years as required by Czech law. All other data is deleted within 90
days of this agreement ending. Bartoň a partneři may export its data at any time, including after
termination.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Neither party limits its liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or
for any other liability that cannot be limited by law.

Subject to that, each party's total liability in any 12-month period is limited to the fees paid
under this agreement during that period.

Neither party is liable for loss of profit, loss of business, or reputational harm.

## 10. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement immediately if the other:

- commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of written notice, or
- becomes insolvent.

Bartoň a partneři may also terminate if Saldo fails to meet the availability target in three
consecutive calendar months.

On termination, Saldo refunds any subscription paid in respect of a period after the termination
date.

## 11. General

This agreement is governed by Czech law, and the courts of the Czech Republic have exclusive
jurisdiction.

It constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes any prior representations or
arrangements.

No variation is effective unless made in writing and signed by both parties.
