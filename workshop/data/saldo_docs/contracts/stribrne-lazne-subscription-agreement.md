## 1. Services

Saldo provides Stříbrné lázně with access to Saldo on the **Professional** plan, for use in
Stříbrné lázně's own business.

Saldo is cloud accounting software. Stříbrné lázně's finance team uses it to keep its books, issue
and chase invoices, and run payroll. Saldo provides the system and applies the statutory rules; the
work is done by Stříbrné lázně.

The subscribed modules are listed in Schedule A. The subscription covers the web application, the
SFTP channel and the API, for entities and employees in the Czech Republic and Slovakia.

The scope of the service, and what falls outside it, is set out in the Service Description
(SALDO-DOC-0001), which forms part of this agreement. Commercial details are set out in
Schedule A.

## 2. Term and Renewal

This agreement runs for **12 months** from the date it comes into force.

It then renews automatically for successive 12-month periods, unless either party gives three
months' written notice before the end of the current period.

## 3. Fees and Payment

Stříbrné lázně pays a monthly subscription based on the number of active employees in the account,
at the rate set out in Schedule A. Saldo invoices monthly in arrears, with payment due within 30
days.

**How active employees are counted.** An employee is *active* in a month if they have a current
contract and were eligible for at least one payroll run in that month. The count is taken from the
employee master on the last day of the month, as set out in the Fee Schedule (SALDO-DOC-0031).

Because Stříbrné lázně's headcount moves substantially between seasons, the following is recorded
for the avoidance of doubt:

- A seasonal employee counts in every month they were eligible to be paid, whether or not they
  were actually paid in that month.
- An employee whose fixed-term contract has ended does not count in later months, provided the end
  date is recorded in the employee master. An end date entered late means the employee continues
  to count until it is entered.
- An employee who leaves and returns in a later season counts in each month they are active. There
  is no re-joining charge.
- The minimum monthly charge applies in every month, including out of season.

Where Stříbrné lázně disputes a count, Saldo provides the underlying list of employees counted for
that month on request, at no charge.

Additional services — off-cycle payroll runs, expedited processing, custom reports — are charged
separately at the rates in Schedule A.

Correction runs are not charged. Where a payroll run leaves employees unpaid and those employees
need to be run again within the same pay period, Saldo makes no charge, regardless of how many
correction runs are required.

Saldo may revise its prices once in any 12-month period, giving three months' notice. If Stříbrné
lázně does not accept the revised prices, it may terminate this agreement with effect from the date
they would take effect.

## 4. Service Levels

Saldo commits to the availability targets set out in its Service Level Agreement. For the
Professional plan this is **99.9% per calendar month**.

Where Saldo fails to meet that target, Stříbrné lázně may claim a service credit against its next
invoice. Credit amounts and the claim process are set out in the Service Level Agreement.

Service credits compensate for the service being unavailable. They do not compensate for the
outcome of an individual payroll run.

Payments rejected because the submitted data did not meet Saldo's published file specification are
the service operating as designed. Such rejections do not count towards unavailability and do not
give rise to a service credit.

## 5. Support

Stříbrné lázně receives support at the Professional level: extended hours, 07:00–19:00 CET on
working days, by email and telephone.

Response targets by severity are set out in Saldo's Support Policy. Saldo assigns severity based on
the facts of each case. Where Stříbrné lázně disagrees with an assigned severity, it may refer the
matter to the duty manager.

## 6. Responsibilities

Saldo is responsible for operating the service, maintaining its availability, processing submitted
data in accordance with the published file specification, and transmitting valid payment
instructions to its banking partner within the agreed timescales.

Stříbrné lázně is responsible for the accuracy of the data it submits, including employee bank
account details and the start and end dates of fixed-term contracts.

Saldo pays what Stříbrné lázně instructs it to pay. Saldo validates that submitted data is well
formed — that an account number is a valid IBAN, that an amount is a positive figure — and rejects
anything that is not. Saldo cannot verify that a correctly formatted account belongs to the
intended recipient. Only Stříbrné lázně is in a position to know that.

Where an employee is paid late because of an error in their record, correcting that record is
Stříbrné lázně's responsibility. Saldo will assist: support can prepare corrected files for
Stříbrné lázně to review, and correction runs are not charged.

A seasonal employee whose contract has expired is not eligible for a run and will not be paid by
it. Extending fixed-term contracts before the period they cover is Stříbrné lázně's
responsibility.

## 7. Changes to the Service

Saldo may change the service during the term, provided it does not reduce the functionality
Stříbrné lázně subscribes to without agreement.

Where a change requires Stříbrné lázně to take action — updating data, altering an integration, or
changing the format of a submitted file — Saldo gives at least **60 days' notice**. Notice is given
by email to Stříbrné lázně's nominated technical contact.

## 8. Data Protection

Stříbrné lázně's data remains its own. Saldo processes it solely to provide the service, and for no
other purpose. Saldo does not sell it and does not use it to train machine learning models.

Saldo acts as processor and Stříbrné lázně as controller. The data processing agreement is attached
as Schedule B.

Stříbrné lázně's guest records include health data, which is processed in its own clinical systems
and not in Saldo. Nothing in this agreement authorises Stříbrné lázně to load special categories of
personal data relating to guests into Saldo.

Payroll records are retained for seven years as required by Czech law. All other data is deleted
within 90 days of this agreement ending. Stříbrné lázně may export its data at any time, including
after termination.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Neither party limits its liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or
for any other liability that cannot be limited by law.

Subject to that, each party's total liability in any 12-month period is limited to the fees paid
under this agreement during that period.

Neither party is liable for loss of profit, loss of business, or reputational harm.

## 10. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement immediately if the other:

- commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of written notice, or
- becomes insolvent.

Stříbrné lázně may also terminate if Saldo fails to meet the availability target in three
consecutive calendar months.

On termination, Saldo refunds any subscription paid in respect of a period after the termination
date.

## 11. General

This agreement is governed by Czech law, and the courts of the Czech Republic have exclusive
jurisdiction.

It constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes any prior representations or
arrangements.

No variation is effective unless made in writing and signed by both parties.
