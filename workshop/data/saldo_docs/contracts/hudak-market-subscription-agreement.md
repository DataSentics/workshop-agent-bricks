## 1. Services

Saldo provides Hudák Market with access to Saldo on the **Standard** plan, for use in Hudák
Market's own business.

Saldo is cloud accounting software. Hudák Market uses it to keep its books and run payroll. Saldo
provides the system and applies the statutory rules; the work is done by Hudák Market.

Hudák Market is a Slovak entity and its payroll is run under Slovak law. Saldo applies Slovak
statutory rates and thresholds to the account and maintains them as they change.

The subscribed modules are listed in Schedule A. The subscription covers the web application and
the SFTP channel. The API is not included on the Standard plan.

The scope of the service, and what falls outside it, is set out in the Service Description
(SALDO-DOC-0001), which forms part of this agreement. Commercial details are set out in
Schedule A.

## 2. Term and Renewal

This agreement runs for **12 months** from the date it comes into force.

It then renews automatically for successive 12-month periods, unless either party gives three
months' written notice before the end of the current period.

## 3. Fees and Payment

Hudák Market pays a monthly subscription based on the number of active employees in the account, at
the rate set out in Schedule A. Saldo invoices monthly in arrears, with payment due within 30 days.

Fees are stated and invoiced in CZK. Hudák Market may settle in EUR at the Czech National Bank rate
published on the invoice date; any difference on conversion is for Hudák Market's account.

Additional services — off-cycle payroll runs, expedited processing, custom reports — are charged
separately at the rates in Schedule A.

Correction runs are not charged. Where a payroll run leaves employees unpaid and those employees
need to be run again within the same pay period, Saldo makes no charge, regardless of how many
correction runs are required.

Saldo may revise its prices once in any 12-month period, giving three months' notice. If Hudák
Market does not accept the revised prices, it may terminate this agreement with effect from the
date they would take effect.

## 4. Service Levels

Saldo commits to the availability targets set out in its Service Level Agreement. For the Standard
plan this is **99.5% per calendar month**.

**The Standard plan does not include service credits.** Where Saldo fails to meet the target, Hudák
Market's remedy is the right to terminate under clause 10, not a credit against its invoice.
Service credits are available on the Professional and Enterprise plans.

Payments rejected because the submitted data did not meet Saldo's published file specification are
the service operating as designed. Such rejections do not count towards unavailability.

## 5. Support

Hudák Market receives support at the Standard level: business hours, 09:00–17:00 CET on working
days, **by email**. Telephone support is available on the Professional and Enterprise plans and is
not included here.

Working days follow the Czech public holiday calendar. Where a Slovak public holiday is a Czech
working day, support is available as normal.

Support outside those hours is charged at the rate in Schedule A.

Response targets by severity are set out in Saldo's Support Policy. Saldo assigns severity based on
the facts of each case. Where Hudák Market disagrees with an assigned severity, it may refer the
matter to the duty manager.

**Onboarding.** For the first three months from the date this agreement comes into force, Saldo
provides onboarding assistance at no charge: loading the opening employee master, checking the
first two payroll runs before submission, and one training session for Hudák Market's staff.

## 6. Responsibilities

Saldo is responsible for operating the service, maintaining its availability, processing submitted
data in accordance with the published file specification, and transmitting valid payment
instructions to its banking partner within the agreed timescales.

Hudák Market is responsible for the accuracy of the data it submits, including employee bank
account details.

Saldo pays what Hudák Market instructs it to pay. Saldo validates that submitted data is well
formed — that an account number is a valid IBAN, that an amount is a positive figure — and rejects
anything that is not. Saldo cannot verify that a correctly formatted account belongs to the
intended recipient. Only Hudák Market is in a position to know that.

Where an employee is paid late because of an error in their record, correcting that record is Hudák
Market's responsibility. Saldo will assist: support can prepare corrected files for Hudák Market to
review, and correction runs are not charged.

**Migrated data.** The employee master was loaded from Hudák Market's previous provider at
onboarding. Saldo loads what it is given. Records that were incomplete in the source remain
incomplete after loading, and Hudák Market is responsible for reviewing the migrated master in
full — not a sample — before its first payroll run. Incomplete records typically surface at the
first run that touches them, which may be months after migration.

## 7. Changes to the Service

Saldo may change the service during the term, provided it does not reduce the functionality Hudák
Market subscribes to without agreement.

Where a change requires Hudák Market to take action — updating data, altering an integration, or
changing the format of a submitted file — Saldo gives at least **60 days' notice**. Notice is given
by email to Hudák Market's nominated technical contact.

## 8. Data Protection

Hudák Market's data remains its own. Saldo processes it solely to provide the service, and for no
other purpose. Saldo does not sell it and does not use it to train machine learning models.

Saldo acts as processor and Hudák Market as controller. The data processing agreement is attached
as Schedule B.

Payroll records are retained for ten years as required by Slovak law. All other data is deleted
within 90 days of this agreement ending. Hudák Market may export its data at any time, including
after termination.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

Neither party limits its liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or
for any other liability that cannot be limited by law.

Subject to that, each party's total liability in any 12-month period is limited to the fees paid
under this agreement during that period.

Neither party is liable for loss of profit, loss of business, or reputational harm.

## 10. Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement immediately if the other:

- commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days of written notice, or
- becomes insolvent.

Hudák Market may also terminate if Saldo fails to meet the availability target in three consecutive
calendar months.

On termination, Saldo refunds any subscription paid in respect of a period after the termination
date.

## 11. General

This agreement is governed by Czech law, and the courts of the Czech Republic have exclusive
jurisdiction. This does not affect the application of Slovak law to Hudák Market's payroll
obligations to its own employees.

It constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes any prior representations or
arrangements.

No variation is effective unless made in writing and signed by both parties.
